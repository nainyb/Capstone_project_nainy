# Capstone_project_nainy
The global transition toward electric vehicles (EVs) is accelerating, driven by environmental concerns, government policies, and technological innovation. However, adoption rates vary significantly across regions, influenced by factors such as income levels, infrastructure availability, and political environments.

This project seeks to analyze EV sales, manufacturers, and charging infrastructure worldwide and compare them with trends in the United States. Specifically, it will:

Examine the relationship between EV sales and median household income.

Evaluate the impact of charging infrastructure availability on EV adoption.

Compare EV adoption in red vs. blue U.S. states, assessing how political environments shape adoption.

Analyze correlations between EV sales and U.S. population distribution.

By addressing these questions, the project aims to uncover key drivers and barriers to EV adoption, providing insights into how socioeconomic, infrastructural, and political factors influence the future of clean transportation.
